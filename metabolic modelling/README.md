# Metabolic Modelling
The metabolic simulations were conducted using the iAF1260 *Escherichia coli* genome-scale metabolic model, which was reconstructed for *E. coli* K-12 substrain MG1655. The SBML model files Ec_iAF1260_flux1.xml and Ec_iAF1260_flux2.xml were obtained from the UCSD Systems Biology Research Group In Silico Organisms repository (https://systemsbiology.ucsd.edu/InSilico Organisms/Ecoli/EcoliSBML, accessed 11.11.2025). The two files differ mainly in the maximum constraint of glucose input, being 8 and 11 mmol/g<sub>DW</sub>/h, respectively.

To estimate the maximum ATP-generating capacity of the metabolic network, the non-growth-associated (ATPM) was selected as the optimisation objective. Under the steady-state assumption of flux balance analysis, intracellular ATP production must equal ATP consumption. Consequently, maximising ATPM provides a proxy for the maximum ATP that can be generated by the metabolic network under the specified environmental constraints. 

The lower bound of the ATPM reaction is fixed in the models at 8.39 mmol/g<sub>DW</sub>/hh, corresponding to the experimentally determined non-growth-associated maintenance (NGAM) requirement. Growth-associated maintenance (GAM) remained incorporated within the biomass reaction, as defined in the original iAF1260 model.
