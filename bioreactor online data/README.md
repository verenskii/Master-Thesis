# Bioreactor online data
Batch cultivations were performed in an Applikon stirred-tank bioreactor. Experiments were conducted at three different pH setpoints (7.0, 8.0, and 9.0) to investigate the effect the pH has on protein production and growth in the two different *E. coli* strains: the original strain (OG) and the strain obtained through adaptive laboratory evolution (ALE).

To assess whether strain performance was maintained upon transfer to a larger cultivation system, batch cultivations of the ALE strain were performed in a 20 L Sartorius stirred-tank bioreactor at a pH setpoint of 7.0.

## Files
| Filename | Type | Content | 
|---|---|---|
| `700mL_online_data.ipynb` | Jupyter Notebook (Python) | Imports and processes online bioreactor data from CSV files, including data selection, cleaning, and calculation of means and standard deviations from triplicate cultivations. Generates two figures with different panel arrangements and consistent formatting for inclusion in the thesis. |
| `700 mL bioreactor online data` | Directory | Contains the raw CSV files with online process data recorded during the 700 mL bioreactor cultivations. | 
| `20L_online_data.ipynb` | Jupyter Notebook (Python) | Imports and processes online bioreactor data from XLS files, including data selection and cleaning, and generates a consistently formatted figure for inclusion in the thesis. |
| `20 L bioreactor online data` | Folder | Contains the raw XLS files with online process data recorded during the 20 L bioreactor cultivations.
