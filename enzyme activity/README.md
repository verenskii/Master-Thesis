# Enzyme activity

### Cultivation Formats
Shake flask cultivations were performed in 200 mL baffled Erlenmeyer flasks containing 50 mL of ATB supplemented with kanamycin (50 µg/mL). Cultures were inoculated to an initial OD<sub>600</sub> of 0.05 and incubated at 25°C with orbital shaking at 150 rpm for 24 h. When recombinant protein expression was induced, IPTG was added 2 h after inoculation to a final concentration of 1 mM. The initial culture pH was adjusted to either 7, 8, or 9 and was not controlled during cultivation. The sampling strategy was defined according to the requirements of each individual experiment.

Batch cultivations (all strain-pH combinations) were performed in an Applikon stirred-tank bioreactor (working volume 700 mL). Experiments were conducted at 25°C and three different pH setpoints (7.0, 8.0, and 9.0). The dissolved oxygen (DO) concentration was controlled at 30% air saturation using an automatic cascade system, in which an initial increase in airflow was followed by an increase in the stirrer speed (300–1000 rpm) once the DO set point was reached. The pH was maintained at the respective set point by automated addition of base (NaOH 1M). Cultures were inoculated to an initial OD<sub>600</sub> of 0.05, and protein expression was induced 2 h after inoculation. Each batch run was carried out for a total duration of 24 h. Sampling was performed immediately before induction, 4, 6, and 8 hours post-inoculation, and at the final endpoint after 24 h.

ALE strain cultivations were performed in a 20 L (working volume) Sartorius stirred-tank bioreactor at 25°C and a pH setpoint of 7.0. The DO concentration was controlled at 30% air saturation using an automatic cascade system, in which an initial increase in airflow was followed by an increase in the stirrer speed (200–1000 rpm) once the DO set point was reached. The pH was maintained at the respective set point by automated addition of base and acid (NaOH 1M, H3PO4 1M). Antifoam was added to the media before fermentation started at a concentration of 0.5%. Cultures were inoculated to an initial OD<sub>600</sub> of 0.05, and protein expression was induced 2 h after inoculation. Each batch run was carried out for a total duration of 24 h. Sampling was performed immediately before induction, 4, 6, and either 8 or 20 hours post-inoculation, and at the final endpoint after 24 h.

### Cell lysis
The final OD<sub>600</sub> of the culture was determined using a spectrometer. Subsequently, the volume required to obtain an OD<sub>600</sub> of 0.1 after resuspension in 1 mL lysis buffer was centrifuged (12000 rpm, 10 min, 4°C) using an Eppendorf Centrifuge 5424 R. Following, the supernatant was removed and the pellet was resuspended in 1 mL lysis buffer. The cells were then lysed mechanically by sonication (Soniprep 150, MSE) using high-frequency sound waves (5 sec ON, 5 sec OFF) for a total active time of 2 min. The soluble lysate was isolated by centrifugation (12000 rpm, 10 min, 4°C) and used for subsequent analysis.

### Specific Kinetic Enzymatic Activity Assays
For the enzymatic activity assay, 190 µL of ADH assay buffer was combined with 10 µL of processed, soluble cell lysate, and absorbance readings were recorded at 340 nm and room temperature for 25 cycles using a CLARIOstar Plus (BMG LABTECH) plate reader. The cycle time depended on the number of samples processed simultaneously and varied between 10 and 14 seconds. The measured change in absorbance in the first 250 sec was used to calculate the volumetric activity after accounting for dilution factors and used volume. Specific enzymatic activities were obtained by normalisation to the corresponding OD<sub>600</sub> measurements.

### Protein quantification
The soluble protein content in the cell lysate was quantified using the BCA Protein Assay Kit (Thermo Scientific; Cat. No. 23250) according to the manufacturer’s official protocol, from the processed soluble cell lysate. Absorbance was measured at 562 nm using a CLARIOstar Plus plate reader (BMG LABTECH).
