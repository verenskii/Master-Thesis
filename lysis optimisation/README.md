# Lysis Optimization

Cell lysis parameters were optimised prior to all analyses.Following preliminary tests using a total of 12 Falcon tube cultures,three shake flask cultivations of the OG strain at pH 7 were conducted to evaluate different cell lysis parameters.The tested parameters included optical densities (OD600) ranging from 0.1 to 19.1, interval times of 5 s and 7 s, and total sonication
On-times of 1 min and 2 min.
